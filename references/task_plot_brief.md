# Task Plot Brief

## Task

- Title: Iowa Gambling Task
- Construct: decision making under uncertainty / reward-loss learning
- Paradigm: repeated four-deck choice with immediate gain/loss feedback and cumulative score.

## Rows

- Disadvantageous decks A/B: higher immediate gain with worse long-term value.
- Advantageous decks C/D: lower immediate gain with better long-term value.
- Timeout: no deck selected before the response deadline.

## Trial Timeline

1. Fixation: 300-500 ms jittered fixation, no response.
2. Decision: 3500 ms. Choose a deck. Press D for A, F for B, J for C, K for D.
3. Feedback: 1100 ms. Show chosen deck, gain, loss, net outcome, and current total, or timeout message.
4. ITI: 300-600 ms jittered fixation, no response.

## Notes

- Decks A/B are disadvantageous; decks C/D are advantageous.
- Initial money is 2000.
- Timeout means no draw and the total score is unchanged.
